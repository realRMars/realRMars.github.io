What this automation app does

01 / CONNECT

The owner connects YouTube

The owner authorises a Google OAuth connection inside a self-hosted n8n instance. There are no public signups or visitor account connections on this website.

02 / CHECK

Verify the destination

A read-only workflow checks the connected channel's ID, name, and custom URL. It helps confirm that a workflow targets Upgrade+ Gaming.

03 / REVIEW

Keep release under control

Video-upload preparation remains a draft. Any future media operation needs the owner's approval for the content, channel, and visibility before execution.

The automation stays local

n8n runs on the owner's computer with persistent local storage. This public website explains the app; it does not expose the workflow editor, accept files, or provide a Google sign-in service.
